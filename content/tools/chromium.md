## Google Sync

On [chromium](https://archlinux.org/packages/?name=chromium), Chrome Sync can be temporarily restored by [using Chrome's OAuth2 credentials](https://gist.github.com/foutrelis/14e339596b89813aa9c37fd1b4e5d9d5) or [getting your own](https://www.chromium.org/developers/how-tos/api-keys), but pay attention to the disclaimers and do not consider this to be a long-term solution.
